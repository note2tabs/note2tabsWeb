## Coverage and changes

All ten languages: English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Arabic, Simplified Chinese, French and Italian. Structural checks cover every public and editor catalog (1,790 public and 986 editor source messages per language), exact placeholders, nonempty values, required literal `delete`, technical MIDI/keyboard tokens and file extensions. Core signup/upload/limit/verification/pricing and navigation phrases were compared across the existing languages, along with suspicious short-label/long-description alignments. This is an engineering and linguistic review, not an independent native-speaker certification of every sentence. A passing coverage check does not prove semantic correctness.

The largest problems were in the unpublished French/Italian machine drafts. The French/Italian public and editor catalogs received extensive contextual corrections and manual rewrites. Corrections include:

- Guitar tablatures versus browser tabs; fingerings, chord voicings, string/fret positions, musical key, timing, bends, slides, hammer-ons and pull-offs.
- Stems as separated audio tracks; drum kick/snare/hi-hat/toms; mute/unmute, copy/paste, fret changes versus speed, and temporal resizing versus musical scales.
- Account deletion instructions now preserve the actual required word `delete`. File extensions, MIDI headers and key identifiers retain their functional literals.
- Signup/login/sign-out failures, verification recovery, upload/download distinctions, account ownership and legal section headings. Removed fabricated European Council/committee text from French headings; Italian liability no longer says reliability.
- Affiliate commissions, payout terminology, invitation messages, missing negation, reminder descriptions, and SEO titles.

Six existing Spanish entries were misaligned: a long editor description appeared as the practice-tools button, and several neighboring labels had the wrong meanings. Corrected those entries and the MP3/WAV converter title.

Pricing duration limits, settings renewal/cancellation text, Premium welcome and affiliate pages now interpolate complete sentences in all ten languages rather than stitching translated English fragments around plan names and counts. Single-month affiliate offers have a dedicated sentence. The analytics locale version is `multilingual-audit-20261016`, allowing comparisons after deployment. No extra user data, consent changes or wider replay scope were added.

French/Italian remain unavailable in production by default. Their errors cannot explain historical conversion differences before release. Blog bodies without authored translations continue to use the documented English fallback with noindex and English canonical; this audit did not author new article translations. Independent native-speaker/legal review has not been performed; this audit does not certify either.

Catalog metrics could not be read with this connection's scopes, so these are exploratory definitions, not approved business metrics.

Editor-only visitors may successfully use the product without any transcription. Compare matching page types and acquisition sources, not just whole-language totals. Blocked attempts separate expected gates (sign-in, file size, credits) from unexpected failures; retries are not unique people or proof of abandonment. Instrumentation is recent, so the 28-day blocked-event window does not imply 28 days of complete coverage.
